Your pieces
Mushrooms

One may sprout on any turn, far from every piece. It blooms two turns later, and the first piece to touch it then chooses: a full heal or a random perk. (Only kills and medals of valor let you pick which perk.) If nobody does within 3 turns, it wilts.

Beetles

Rhinoceros beetles, three times a piece's size with 150 HP, each guard a hill. A beetle charges any piece within 50 mm, ploughing straight through and doing 25 damage to everything it runs into (a ball takes none), and it chases whoever shoots it. It gives up a chase once it is 100 mm outside its territory and heads home. It heals 5 HP a turn. Hurting one earns no medals; killing one earns 3 perks.

Hills and valleys

The ground rises up to 15 mm and dips down to 10 mm, shown by contour lines every 1 mm (a darker one every 5 mm). A cannon reaches 100 mm plus the shooter's height minus the target's: from a 10 mm hill it reaches 110 mm onto level ground and 120 mm into a 10 mm valley, but only 90 mm from level ground up onto that hill. The flame's reach stays 75 mm.

The ice

After turn 7 a random rock turns to ice. Every turn after that the ice spreads: by default 22 mm a turn while it covers under half the globe, 18 mm up to 80%, then 14 mm (set with the Ice spread sliders). Any piece it reaches, or that moves onto it, takes 35 damage, once a turn, and freezes if that leaves it at 0. The pale band shows where it will reach next. In the end it covers the whole sphere.

Medals of valor

A piece earns a medal each time it damages an enemy piece, and each time it blocks an enemy attack by being rolled into a ball. Every 3 medals, it picks a new perk.

Perks

A piece that destroys an enemy piece earns a perk of your choice, from the ones it doesn't have yet. It keeps it for the rest of the game. Pick the piece at the start of a turn to switch perks on for that turn, before giving its order. A gold health tag means a piece has perks.
