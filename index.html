Your pieces
Mushrooms

One may sprout on any turn, far from every piece. It blooms two turns later, and the first piece to touch it then chooses: a full heal or a random perk. (Only kills and medals of valor let you pick which perk.) If nobody does within 3 turns, it wilts.

The ice

After turn 7 a random rock turns to ice. Every turn after that the ice spreads 25 mm, and any piece it reaches, or that moves onto it, freezes and dies. The pale band shows where it will reach next. In the end it covers the whole sphere.

Medals of valor

A piece earns a medal each time it damages an enemy piece, and each time it blocks an enemy attack by being rolled into a ball. Every 3 medals, it picks a new perk.

Perks

A piece that destroys an enemy piece earns a perk of your choice, from the ones it doesn't have yet. It keeps it for the rest of the game. Pick the piece at the start of a turn to switch perks on for that turn, before giving its order. A gold health tag means a piece has perks.
